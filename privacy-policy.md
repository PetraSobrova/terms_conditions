# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Home Pilates provides guided exercise demonstrations, personalized workout planning, and workout tracking.

This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## At a glance

| Topic | What we do |
| --- | --- |
| Account | No account is required for core use |
| Storage | Most profile and workout data stays on your device |
| Purchases | Subscriptions are handled by Apple and our subscription provider |
| Analytics | The app does not include a third-party product-analytics SDK |
| Sharing | We do not sell personal information |
| Tracking | The app is not designed to track you across other companies’ apps or websites |

## Information we handle

Depending on how you use the app, we may handle:

- onboarding answers such as age range, height, weight, target weight, goal, workout style, experience, body focus, and Pilates level;
- workout progress, completed sessions, skipped sessions, and in-progress workout checkpoints;
- subscription and purchase status, including whether Pro access is active or restored;
- support messages you send to us;
- device and app information needed to run the app, show subscription status, and troubleshoot problems.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- build and update your personalized workout schedule;
- remember your progress and resume interrupted workouts;
- show the correct locked or unlocked workout content;
- process purchases, renewals, and restorations;
- respond to support requests;
- keep the app secure and functioning properly.

## Local storage

Your profile answers, plan preferences, workout history, and checkpoint information are stored locally on your device using the app’s storage.

If you delete the app, those locally stored details are removed from the device unless you restore them from a backup or an Apple-managed restore process.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled through Apple’s in-app purchase system and our subscription provider.

When you subscribe or restore a purchase, we may receive status information such as whether access is active, whether a plan was restored, and related entitlement details. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Apple and the subscription provider may process purchase history, entitlement state, and related technical information needed to manage the subscription.

## Support

If you contact us for help, we may use the information you send to answer your request and keep a record of the conversation for support purposes.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only when needed to:

- operate the app and its subscription features;
- comply with law or valid legal requests;
- protect the app, our users, or our rights;
- work with service providers that help us run the app.

Any service provider we use is expected to handle data only for the work we ask them to do.

## Product analytics

This app is not designed to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

The shipping app does not include a third-party product-analytics, feature-flag, or crash-reporting SDK. Onboarding answers such as age, height, weight, target weight, movement limitations, and body-focus selections stay on your device unless you choose to include them in a support message you send to us.

## Retention

We keep local workout and profile data on your device until you remove it.

Support messages may be retained for a reasonable period so we can follow up and keep basic records of our communications.

Subscription and purchase-related records may be retained by Apple and our subscription provider according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## Your choices

You can:

- delete the app to remove locally stored app data from your device;
- manage subscriptions through your Apple account settings;
- contact us if you want help understanding what the app stores locally;
- contact us if you have questions about the information stored on your device.

## Children

This app is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required by local law if higher.

## Third-party services

The app relies on third-party services including Apple and RevenueCat for purchases and subscription status.

Those services have their own privacy practices and terms. We recommend reviewing them separately.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by the app. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make meaningful changes, we will update the effective date and may show the updated version in the app or on our website.
